Professional Danger Zone

Restricted operational actions for PRIMARY_OWNER only. Destructive actions require 4-step confirmation (Warning, Text Verification, Password Auth, Execution).

1. Delete Farm

Permanently delete this farm and ALL associated operational records in a single transaction.

• Farm Profile• Chickens• Workers• Egg Records• Health Records• Feed Records• Finance Records• Reports• Settings

2. Remove All Chicken Data

Deletes chicken profiles, QR codes, photos, health history, growth history, and egg history associated with chickens. Does NOT delete farm, workers, or finance.

3. Remove All Egg Production Data

Deletes daily oviposition egg records, egg batches, monthly yield logs, and egg analytics.

4. Remove All Health Records

Deletes vaccination doses, medical treatment logs, diagnostic histories, and follow-up reminders.

5. Remove All Feed Records

Deletes daily feed consumption logs, feed purchase inventory entries, feed items, and supplier profiles.

6. Remove All Financial Records

Deletes income entries, expense entries, cashbook accounts, sales order receipts, and financial reports.

7. Remove All Reports

Deletes generated report snapshots and summary cache files only. Raw operational logs remain intact.

8. Reset Farm Settings

Restores default notification thresholds, theme preferences, and dashboard layout. Does NOT delete farm operational data.

9. Export Complete Farm Backup

Generates a complete downloadable JSON/ZIP backup file containing Farm, Chickens, Workers, Egg Records, Health, Feed, Finance, and Settings.

10. Import Farm Backup

Restores farm configuration and operational records from an exported backup file. Validates file integrity before import.
